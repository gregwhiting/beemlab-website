Outreach

In the BEEM Lab we highly value outreach to local K-12 schools! We organize STEM activities and events with K-12 schools in the Denver/Boulder area. With classroom visits, CU Boulder tours, keynote speeches, and science fairs we strive to increase access and engagement to STEM and higher education for local K-12 students!

If you are interested in getting involved in K-12 outreach, please contact: nicole.luna@colorado.edu
